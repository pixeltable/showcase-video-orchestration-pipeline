# Video evidence workspace

## Platform

Local web application, backed by a temporary Pixeltable catalog. The existing command-line
lesson and optional research comparisons remain available.

## Audience and task

Developers and researchers inspecting how source media, preparation, business rules, and
stored query results fit together. The workspace makes the existing zero-key lesson
interactive: preview its generated clip, prepared frames and audio; change brightness
eligibility; refresh one request; inspect actual preparation and evaluation counts.

## Product truth

The local workspace makes no provider calls and accepts no uploads. One generated source
is prepared once and reused by two requests. Query-backed evidence stays stored until an
explicit refresh. The optional paid comparisons have separate admission and accounting
controls; completed provider calls do not establish truthful answers.

## Scope and limits

Bind to the loopback interface in a new temporary home. Do not present this local lesson as
an authenticated hosted product, a research quality score, or a general workflow builder.
Historical research data and paper artifacts remain unchanged.
